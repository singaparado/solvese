The Premise Audit

Legibility Score reads the sentence. This reads the premise underneath it.

Clean copy can still rest on a broken assumption. This tool detects the specific signals — an unqualified category label, a mismatched authority cue, an unstated buyer belief — and tells you exactly which judgment call is yours to make next.

A NOTE ON WHAT THIS ACTUALLY DOES: This tool detects patterns mechanically — it cannot know your market the way a human reading your competitors and your buyers can. Every "trigger fired" below is a real, detected signal in your text. The interpretation of what that signal means for your specific business is a judgment call, stated plainly as a question for you to answer. This is intentional. A tool that pretended to have all the answers here would be lying to you.
