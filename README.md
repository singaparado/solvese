# Solvese

Solvese is a strategic communication advisory that helps organizations operating in complex domains become coherent, legible, and credible to the people whose trust they need — buyers, investors, regulators, partners, and the public.

Solvese's market is not defined by industry. It is defined by complexity. Clients span technology, infrastructure, healthcare, biotechnology, climate technology, manufacturing, GovTech, research institutions, and public institutions — any organization whose internal sophistication has outpaced its external legibility.

This repository contains Solvese's website and proprietary diagnostic tools.

---

# Mission

Reduce complexity without reducing sophistication.

Solvese exists because engineering, scientific, or operational excellence alone rarely determines whether an organization is understood, trusted, or adopted. As organizations scale, communication often fails to evolve at the same pace — internal language fragments, positioning drifts, and the outside world is left assembling an inaccurate picture of what the organization actually does.

Solvese exists to close that gap, without ever asking the organization to become simpler than it actually is.

---

# What Solvese Does

Solvese operates on the external expression of an organization — messaging architecture, positioning, communication systems, investor and sales communication, organizational language, terminology consistency, and strategic narrative.

The objective is never "better copy." The objective is organizational coherence as perceived by the people outside the organization.

## Engagement Tiers

- **Scans** — The Narrative Audit. A structured diagnosis of where current messaging breaks down.
- **Sketches** — The Narrative Blueprint. A complete positioning and messaging architecture.
- **Sculpts** — The Narrative Imprint. Communication rebuilt across every touchpoint.
- **Scribes** — The Narrative Retainer. Ongoing calibration as the organization and market evolve.

## Diagnostic Tools

- **Legibility Score** — free, client-side diagnostic scoring hero copy against the Three Laws of Translation (Tangibility, Intuitiveness, Sustainability) and the Interpretation Window.
- **Premise Audit** — reads the assumptions beneath the copy: category assumptions, authority mismatches, and audience-model gaps that clean writing alone can hide.

---

# Proprietary Frameworks

- **TRACE** — Tracing Relational Authority and Cultural Expectations. The discovery methodology: maps the uncodified authority hierarchies, symbolic economies, and relational friction points that govern how an audience evaluates and trusts before a word of communication is written.
- **FORGE** — Framework for Operational Recognition through Grounded Expression. The construction methodology: builds the language and interface layer on top of what TRACE maps.
- **AOS** — Adaptive Ontology System. A structural intelligence layer that keeps meaning, systems, and perception synchronized as an organization scales.

RAFU and ASUKSHIRA are separate, related frameworks developed by Waysoroh (Mahdi's cultural strategy practice) and are not Solvese's intellectual property. The two should never be conflated.

# Relationship to Zordere

Solvese and Zordere are two separate companies and should never be blurred together.

**Solvese** is the applied, revenue-generating consultancy — the practice in this repository, running client engagements and the diagnostic tools above.

**Zordere** is a separate, longer-horizon software company building an Organizational Coherence Infrastructure platform — an "Organizational Model Builder" that represents an organization's products, decisions, frameworks, and research as a relationship-aware graph, with an "AI Organizational Coherence Audit" as its first flagship product.

The working relationship: Solvese discovers patterns through real client work. Zordere encodes those patterns into software. Neither is a subsidiary or a rebrand of the other.
